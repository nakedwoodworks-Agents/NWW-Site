# PersonalizationMall: what to copy (structure, not design or text)

Pulled live 2026-09-27 from personalizationmall.com (homepage + the /Personalized-Cutting-Boards-d2840.dept page). Earlier teardown numbers are from Jackson's July 2026 PMall workup.

## Their navigation model
- Top level: **For Her · For Him · For Baby · For Kids · [current season] · Wedding · Birthday · Kitchenware · Bar & Wine · Outdoor**
- Mega-menus split every audience **three ways: By Category · By Recipient · By Interest**
- Four "shop by" indexes: **Shop By Occasion · Shop By Recipient · Shop By Product · Shop By Sale**
- Wedding has sub-recipients: Bride, Groom, Parents, Bridesmaids, Groomsmen, Flower Girl, Ring Bearer, Favors
- Kitchenware splits into Cutting Boards · Bar Glassware · Coasters · Decanters · Serving Platters, which maps almost 1:1 to NWW's catalog
- A "Corporate Gifts" link sits in the utility bar (top right), always visible
- Coupon ladder banner at the very top: $5 off $35 / $10 off $50 / $15 off $75 / $20 off $90. **Don't copy the discount itself.** Offers are Jackson's call.
- BreadcrumbList schema on category pages; ~52,000 programmatic SEO pages (July teardown). Their segment revenue fell 18-23% and they cited "changes to search engine results pages," which is a warning about thin programmatic pages.

## What NWW copies
1. The **three-way browse** (occasion / recipient / product type) on every hub, via the taxonomy.json tags.
2. **Mega-menu** with occasions leading (Anniversary first, the quiz's revenue engine), then Wedding, Housewarming, Realtor & Corporate, Christmas.
3. **Corporate/Business link always visible** in the header utility bar.
4. Dense product grids (4-up desktop, 2-up mobile) with name, "from $X", and a small proof line where real ("5,500+ sold" only where `etsy_sales_all_time` supports it).
5. Wedding sub-recipients as filters (bride, groom, parents, bridesmaids) where products exist.

## What NWW does NOT copy
- Licensed sports/brands (41% of PMall's board/glass catalog per the July teardown). NWW can't sell those.
- Always-on fake-urgency sale banners (against NWW brand voice: "no countdowns, no scarcity").
- Their visual design, photos and copy. NWW's look is **white / black / metallic gold** (reference/NWW-brand-voice.md → Visual pairing).

## Gaps PMall leaves open (from the July teardown; NWW should lead with these)
- Charcuterie: PMall has only 11 charcuterie boards vs 254 cutting boards.
- **Zero board + glass bundles.** NWW sells board + coaster sets already. Surface "gift sets" prominently.
- Glassware is sold as singles at PMall. NWW has sets of 2/4/8.
