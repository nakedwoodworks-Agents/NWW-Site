## What makes a closing gift stay in the house

Plenty of closing gifts get used once and forgotten: a bottle of wine, a gift card, a basket. They are fine, but nothing about them connects back to the house or to you. The gifts that stay tend to share a few traits:

- **Their names are on it.** A gift with the buyers' names and the year they bought the house belongs to them in a way a generic item never does.
- **It gets used or seen.** A cutting board lives on the counter. Every time friends come over, it is part of the kitchen.
- **It is not an ad.** If your branding is small or absent, the buyers keep the gift out. If it is large, it goes in a drawer.
- **It is sturdy.** A solid wood board does not break in the move, and it will make the next move with them too.

## Timing and logistics

Every board is made to order in 3-5 business days, and shipping time comes on top of that. A few habits make it easier:

- Order when the contract goes pending, not the week of closing. You have the names by then.
- Keep a clean logo file handy so you are not hunting for it every time. A vector file is best; a high-resolution image works too.
- If a closing moves up, ask us about rush.
- Check the free digital proof carefully. Names and dates are where mistakes happen, and it is much easier to fix them before we engrave.
