Each wedding anniversary has a traditional gift material, starting with paper for the first year and running to diamond for the sixtieth, and there is a separate modern list with more everyday items. Wood is the traditional 5th and the modern 6th, and those are the years we make the most sense for: we engrave maple and walnut boards, coasters, glasses and ornaments, made to order in Pryor, Oklahoma.

## A note before the list

Anniversary lists vary by source. The traditional list goes back a long way and has been added to over time. The modern list is newer and differs from one publisher to the next, especially after year 15. Treat both as a menu of ideas, not a rule. If your spouse follows one list closely, go with that one.

## The list by year

Year: traditional, then modern.

- **1st:** paper; clocks
- **2nd:** cotton; china
- **3rd:** leather; crystal or glass
- **4th:** fruit or flowers; appliances
- **5th:** wood; silverware
- **6th:** iron or sugar; wood
- **7th:** wool or copper; desk sets
- **8th:** bronze; linens or lace
- **9th:** pottery or willow; leather
- **10th:** tin or aluminum; diamond jewelry
- **11th:** steel; fashion jewelry
- **12th:** silk or linen; pearls
- **13th:** lace; textiles or furs
- **14th:** ivory; gold jewelry
- **15th:** crystal; watches
- **20th:** china; platinum
- **25th:** silver; silver
- **30th:** pearl; diamond
- **35th:** coral or jade; jade
- **40th:** ruby; ruby
- **45th:** sapphire; sapphire
- **50th:** gold; gold
- **55th:** emerald; emerald
- **60th:** diamond; diamond

From year 25 on, the two lists mostly agree. The early years are where they split.

## Choosing a gift when the material does not appeal

Some years are hard. Few people want a tin gift at ten years or a set of appliances at four. A few approaches that work:

- Follow the other list for that year.
- Take the material loosely. Iron for the 6th can be a cast iron pan; sugar can be a favorite dessert.
- Pair a small themed gift with something personal you would have chosen anyway.

Engraved gifts are good for that last approach, because the personal part is built in. Your names and date make it about the two of you, whatever year it is.

## What to put on an anniversary gift

Keep the engraving to what will still be true in twenty years: names, the shared last name or an initial, and the wedding date. Add a year count only if you want the gift tied to this specific anniversary.
