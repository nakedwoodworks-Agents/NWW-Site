We engrave business gifts with your logo on solid maple and walnut boards and wood ornaments, made to order in our shop in Pryor, Oklahoma. Companies send them to clients, give them to employees, and use them to mark milestones like five or twenty years on the job.

- **Logo ornaments.** An engraved wood [Custom Logo Ornament](/p/custom-logo-ornament/), from $16.60, sold single or in sets of 2, 5 or 10. A good fit for a holiday card list.
- **Years-of-service boards.** A name, a number of years and your logo on the [Years of Service Appreciation Board](/p/years-of-service-appreciation-board/), for retirements and work anniversaries.

## The boards themselves

Every board is one solid slab of maple or walnut. Maple is light and hard, and the engraving shows up dark and crisp. Walnut is a rich dark brown and looks at home on a desk or a kitchen counter. Sizes are 8x12 and 10x14 inches (0.75 inch thick), 12x16 inches (1.5 inches thick), and a 16x24 inch walnut board (1.5 inches thick). Add-ons include a display stand, conditioning oil, a handwritten card and paper gift wrap.

## Getting your logo right

Logos are where business orders go wrong, so it is worth a minute here. A vector file (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) is best, because it stays sharp at any size. A high-resolution PNG or JPG works as well. Screenshots and images pulled from a website are usually too small. Engraving is one color burned into the wood, so gradients and fine shading turn into solid areas. A simple one-color version of your logo often engraves better than the full-color one.

You upload the file on the product page. Our designer places it and sends you a free digital proof before anything is engraved. You approve the layout, the spelling and the size, and then we cut.

## Timing and planning

Each piece is made to order in 3-5 business days, then it ships. For a larger order, the proof and the list of names take their own time, so build that in. Holiday gifts are the most common business order, and the easiest ones are the ones where we know the quantity and date early. If you are up against a date, ask us about rush.
