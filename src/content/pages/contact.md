This is how to reach Naked Wood Works with a question about an order, a design or a date. Messages are read and answered by the people in our Pryor, Oklahoma shop, as people, not scripts.

## What to include

A few details help us answer on the first reply:

- **Your order number**, if you have already ordered.
- **The names, dates or wording** you want engraved, spelled exactly as they should appear.
- **The date you need it by**, if there is one. If it is tight, ask us about rush.
- **A photo**, if something arrived damaged or does not look right.

## Realtor and business gifts

Real estate agents can start with our [realtors page](/realtors/) or our dedicated realtor site, [Forever Client Gifts](https://foreverclientgifts.com). For logo gifts for clients or employees, the [business page](/business/) has an inquiry form where you can tell us your quantity and date.

Many common questions, about proofs, sizes, care and timing, are answered on our [FAQ](/faq/) and [shipping](/shipping/) pages.
